About Our College

Aditya College of Engineering and Technology is one of the leading engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh. It provides quality education, experienced faculty, and modern laboratories to help students build strong technical skills.

The college ensures students to participate in internships, workshops, coding competitions, and placement training. It offers a good learning environment and excellent career opportunities.
